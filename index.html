THE 120-DAY CURRICULUM
Click phase to expand
MONTH 0
BASELINE ASSESSMENT & INSTALLATION
Days 1–30 · Learn
The host gives you one month to learn the 14 rewires before the 90-day application sprint. This phase is your intake — diagnosing your nervous system, identifying whether your blocks are biochemical, structural, or psychological, and installing your new identity before you have evidence it exists.
PHASE 1
IDENTITY FRICTION
Days 31–60 · Apply
The first application month is categorised by identity friction. Around Day 21 of this phase (Day 51 overall), you will hit a wall — doubt, impostor syndrome, resistance. This is NOT a signal to stop. The nervous system is fighting to preserve the old identity. That resistance is a reverse indicator you are on the right path. Push through with protocol discipline.
PHASE 2
PATTERN STABILIZATION
Days 61–90 · Stabilize
By Day 45 of active application (Day 75 overall), you will start making bold decisions and speaking with conviction your old self would have avoided. Patterns stabilize. The biochemical dominance loop runs more automatically. Identity begins to feel natural rather than performed.
PHASE 3
IDENTITY TIPPING POINT
Days 91–120 · Cement
By Day 90 of application, neural patterns are cemented. You will look back at who you were and not recognise the posture, tone, or operating system. The new identity is no longer performed — it is automatic. This is where the foundation locks in permanently.
∞ ONGOING
CONTINUOUS CONDITIONING
Day 121+ · Forever
The curriculum doesn't end. After the foundation is wired, you enter continuous conditioning — progressively harder chaos exposure, performance measurement under increasing pressure, and identity expansion. Low stakes → medium stakes → high stakes. The nervous system is always either growing or shrinking. There is no neutral.

DAILY TRAINING RITUALS
Non-negotiable daily architecture
MORNING — 11 TO 15 MIN
PRIMING SEQUENCE
  • 01Sky Before Screens. No phone. 16oz water + electrolytes. Delay caffeine 34 min. Walk outside, look at sky. Sets circadian rhythm, triggers healthy cortisol + dopamine.
  • 02Fascia Activation. Barefoot on grass if possible. Skips, pogo jumps, 2 sprints. Awakens foot complex, activates toe-to-glute neural connection.
  • 0315x15 Identity Install. Mirror. Power posture. Repeat "I am" statement 15x with intense emotion. Not an affirmation — a declaration. A command.
  • 04Visualization. 3 min eyes closed. Vividly feel yourself dominating your goals 30 days to 3 years out. Brain cannot distinguish real from vividly imagined.
NIGHT — 10 TO 15 MIN
RECONSTRUCTION SEQUENCE
  • 01Triple D Review. 3 min exactly. What did you Do, Defer, Delete today? Closes open cognitive loops (Zeigarnik effect). Drops cortisol for sleep.
  • 0215x15 Night Install. Mirror again. Repeat identity statement 15x. Brain is most subconscious-connected at sleep onset — highest neuroplasticity window.
  • 03Tomorrow's 3 Priorities. Physical pen on paper. Three needle-movers only. Eliminates morning decision fatigue. Pre-loads the RAS overnight.
  • 04Sleep Architecture. Screens off 90 min prior. Pitch black, cold room. GABA + magnesium glycinate 300mg + L-theanine 1hr before bed. Engineers slow-wave REM.
INTRA-DAY
PERFORMANCE PROTOCOLS
  • →Deep Work Blocks. 90 min time-boxed sessions. Min 2x per week. No context switching. Batch all notifications.
  • →Post-Meal Movement. 10–20 min walk after every meal. Blunts glucose spike. Preserves prefrontal cortex function.
  • →Micro-Win Log. Track 5–10 small wins per day in a performance journal. Provides nervous system undeniable proof of success. Engineers confidence through data.
  • →DAD Protocol. Every task: Delete → Automate → Delegate → Do. Protects cognitive bandwidth from decision fatigue.
WEEKLY STRUCTURE
LOAD MANAGEMENT
  • ↑High-Output Days. Micro-dose strength training 10–20 min before performance. Trap bar jumps, fast cleans. Higher carbs (2g/kg). Prime the CNS without fatiguing it.
  • ↓Recovery Days. 20–30 min "motion is lotion" mobility. Zone 2 walks. Barefoot grounding. Lower carbs (0.5g/kg), higher healthy fats and anti-inflammatory foods.
  • ⚡Barefoot Training. 1–2 sessions/week completely barefoot. Strengthens toe-to-glute neural connection. Do NOT static stretch before — inhibits muscle function.
  • 📊HRV Check. Daily Oura/Whoop reading. If HRV is low: switch to recovery protocols. Fastest way to improve anything is to measure it.
